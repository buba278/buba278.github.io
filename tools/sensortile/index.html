how to

  • Run it on battery with USB unplugged, phone and laptop well away from the board.
  • Stand away from steel, ideally where the board will be used.
  • In each position, turn the board slowly through one full circle, about 10 seconds.
  • The positions are z up, z down, and standing on each of its four edges. The page works out which one you are in from the accelerometer.
  • One position per axis is enough to apply. All six give a steadier result.
  • Field spread is how much the field strength varies over the capture. It should fall to a few percent once calibrated.
